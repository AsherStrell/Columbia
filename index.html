      Identification Guide

      Has Singing

      Italian opera, lush soprano
      Puccini — Mi Chiamano Mimì (La bohème)
      Lyrical soaring soprano, late-Romantic harmony, single voice with orchestra. Anna Netrebko.
      Spanish-flavored mezzo, habanera rhythm
      Bizet — Habanera (Carmen)
      DUM dum-DUM dum habanera rhythm, descending chromatic line. Maria Callas.
      English-language opera, AfAm-inflected
      William Grant Still — Troubled Island
      Sung in English, Caribbean / blues color. About Haiti overthrowing the French.
      German opera, leitmotifs, huge orchestra
      Wagner — Walküre / Tristan
      Brass-heavy, recurring leitmotifs, never-ending melody, no closed numbers. Gesamtkunstwerk. Tristan Chord on Liebestod.
      Atonal opera, speech-song
      Berg — Wozzeck (Act III)
      Sprechstimme, dissonant, claustrophobic — "weird singing over weird harmony." Sc.2 = pond/murder; Sc.4 = drowning; Sc.5 = children playing.
      1950s American musical, snappy syncopation
      Bernstein — West Side Story
      Tritone on "Maria," general dissonance, jazz-flavored orchestration, gang chase music.
      Jazz vocal, blue notes, swing feel
      Gershwin — Porgy & Bess (My Man's Gone Now / Bess You Is My Woman Now)
      Lowered 3rds/5ths/7ths, swung rhythms. Bess "you is" sung in blue notes.
      Ragtime opera, syncopated piano under voice
      Joplin — Treemonisha (Aunt Dinah)
      Vocal music with ragtime piano underneath. Aunt Dinah opens with horn — sounds cult-ish.

      Orchestral, No Singing

      Pentatonic, English-horn solo, "Goin' Home"
      Dvořák — New World Symphony, Largo
      Five-note pentatonic theme, slow, melancholy. Native-American–inspired.
      Dreamy flute, tritones, no clear meter
      Debussy — Prelude to the Afternoon of a Faun
      Opens with hazy flute solo, Impressionist, sudden major↔minor shifts.
      Brass fanfare, galloping triplets
      Wagner — Ride of the Valkyries
      French horns, triplet rhythm, heroic. Ice-Age-squirrel iconic theme.
      Pounding ostinato, savage rhythm
      Stravinsky — The Rite of Spring
      Repeated dissonant chords (ostinato), polytonality (two keys at once), irregular meter, percussive.
      Atonal, fragmented, sparse
      Schoenberg — Variations for Orchestra Op. 31
      No tonal center, 12-tone row, Second Viennese School. Creepy with no singing.
      Open intervals, "Simple Gifts" wholesome
      Copland — Appalachian Spring
      Open 4ths/5ths, plains/cowboy America. Doppio = upbeat striding; Moderato = whole symphony together.

      Jazz / Solo Piano

      Old-timey cornet, upbeat, "Mickey Mouse music"
      Louis Armstrong — Cornet Chop Suey
      Sounds like a saxophone in old music, super upbeat, improvised. Inventor of jazz.
      Smooth dance band, muted brass, train-track vibe
      Duke Ellington — Mood Indigo
      Slow swing, lush horns (clarinet, muted trumpet, banjo), trippy night-time feel.
      Romantic tenor sax ballad
      Coleman Hawkins — Body and Soul
      Opens with beautiful piano, then long sax improvisation. Romantic-movie opener vibe.
      Fast bebop sax, lightning runs
      Charlie Parker — Cherokee
      Bebop — fast complex jazz of the 1940s. Notable saxophone solo.
      Modal sax improvisation
      Coltrane — Summertime
      Saxophone dominates. Black-tie-event-background-music feel.
      Stride piano, syncopated right hand
      Joplin — Maple Leaf Rag
      Top and bottom go against each other (syncopation). Made his career; turns ragtime into art.
      Famous clarinet glissando opening
      Gershwin — Rhapsody in Blue
      Ragtime + blues fusion. Piano + jazz band.

      Quick Review Q&A

      Late Romantic Opera composers?
      Puccini, Bizet, William Grant Still
      Puccini's piece?
      Mi Chiamano Mimì (La bohème) — Anna Netrebko
      Bizet's piece?
      Habanera (Carmen) — Maria Callas
      Still's piece?
      Selection from Troubled Island — Quanda Johnson
      Wagner's operas in this unit?
      Die Walküre, Tristan und Isolde
      Walküre selections?
      Prelude/Storm, Siegmund & Sieglinde Meet, Spring Song, Siegmund & the Sword, Ride of the Valkyries
      Tristan selection?
      Liebestod aria
      What is Gesamtkunstwerk?
      "Total artwork" — Wagner's union of music, drama, poetry, staging into a single work
      What is the Tristan Chord?
      F-B-D♯-G♯ — opens Tristan, suspended, never resolves on time. Recurs throughout.
      What is a leitmotif? ★
      Recurring musical theme tied to a character or idea (Wagner)
      Late Romantic / Early Modern composers?
      Dvořák, Debussy
      Dvořák's piece?
      New World Symphony, 2nd Mvt (Largo)
      Debussy's piece?
      Prelude to the Afternoon of a Faun
      Pentatonic piece? ★
      Dvořák New World Largo (5-note scale, no half-steps, folkish)
      Tritone piece? ★
      Debussy Faun (also Bernstein "Maria" — three whole steps, dreamy/ominous)
      High Modernism composer?
      Stravinsky
      Stravinsky's work?
      The Rite of Spring — Introduction, Girls' Dance, Spring Rounds, Procession of the Oldest, Glorification, Sacrificial Dance
      Ostinato piece? ★
      Stravinsky Rite of Spring (especially Spring Rounds — slow 4-beat repeated figure)
      What is polytonality?
      Two or more keys sounded simultaneously (Stravinsky's Girls' Dance superimposes two key chords)
      Second Viennese School composers?
      Schoenberg, Berg (and Webern)
      Schoenberg's piece?
      Variations for Orchestra Op. 31 (Introduction-Theme, Var. 1, Var. 2)
      Berg's opera?
      Wozzeck — Act III scenes 2 (Note), 4 (Chord), 5 (Regular Rhythm)
      12-tone piece? ★
      Schoenberg Variations for Orchestra (uses all 12 chromatic pitches in a row, no repeats)
      Atonal pieces? ★
      Schoenberg Variations, Berg Wozzeck (no tonal center, no home key)
      What is Sprechstimme?
      Speech-song — between speaking and singing, slides up and down in pitch (Schoenberg, Berg)
      What is Expressionism?
      Early-20c style — extreme emotion, distortion, atonality. Berg wrote Wozzeck during WWI.
      American Modern composers?
      Copland, Bernstein
      Copland's piece?
      Appalachian Spring — Doppio movimento + Moderato
      Bernstein's piece?
      West Side Story — Prologue, Jet Song, A Boy Like That / I Have a Love
      Jazz figures?
      Armstrong, Ellington, Hawkins, Parker, Coltrane
      Armstrong's piece?
      Cornet Chop Suey (improvised; "inventor of jazz")
      Ellington's piece?
      Mood Indigo (clarinet + muted trumpet + banjo)
      Hawkins's piece?
      Body and Soul
      Parker's piece?
      Cherokee (bebop)
      Coltrane's piece?
      Summertime
      What is bebop?
      Fast, complex jazz of the 1940s — notable saxophone solos (Parker, Gillespie)
      What are blue notes?
      Lowered 3rd, 5th, 7th — bent pitches characteristic of blues / jazz
      American Fusion composers?
      Joplin, Gershwin
      Joplin's pieces?
      Maple Leaf Rag, Treemonisha (Aunt Dinah Has Blowed the Horn)
      What is Treemonisha about?
      Ragtime opera — black town, education, woman trying to get an education. Some ragtime, not 100%.
      Gershwin's pieces?
      Porgy & Bess (My Man's Gone Now, Bess You Is My Woman Now), Rhapsody in Blue
      What is Porgy and Bess?
      Gershwin opera set in low-income Charleston SC, observed black folk music. About a beggar and a heroin addict.
      Syncopation piece? ★
      Joplin Maple Leaf Rag (top + bottom against each other; also all jazz)
      Bonus: Cook's piece?
      Rare Swing Along recording — old, blues + jazzy, AfAm music
      Bonus: Dawson's piece?
      Negro Folk Symphony — opens sad, turns triumphant; brass (horn) opening
      Bonus: Modern Broadway pieces?
      Warm All Over (Most Happy Fella), How I Saved Roosevelt (Assassins), Jerry Likes My Corn
